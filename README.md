# Create: Apparel

Create: Apparel is a modern fashion addon for Create, bringing automated clothing production and customizable outfits to Minecraft.

- Turn fibers into thread, weave and dye fabrics, and sew clothes using Create-powered machines.
- Mix and match clothing, shoes, and accessories in your Wardrobe, with colors, patterns, silhouettes, and layering options.
- Display your outfits with mannequins, hangers, clothing racks, and mirrors.
- Bring your town to life with Citizen NPCs, clothing shops, fashion photography, and advertisements.

For Minecraft 1.20.1 with Forge. Requires Create 6.0.8 or later. Create: Destroy is optional and adds synthetic material production routes.
